## Where it goes

| Priority | What it pays for |
|---|---|
| **Winter resilience for seniors** | Battery backup for older residents' homes, so furnaces, heat-pump fans, medical devices and refrigerators keep running during outages. Where utility rates allow, batteries can also lower bills. |
| **Fire & EMS** | Equipment and training that local fire companies and EMS squads request. Many are volunteer-run and fundraising constantly. |
| **Local backup power** | Grid-tied battery storage at shared sites, like fire stations, community centers and warming shelters, to keep the community running when the grid goes down. |

Neighbors and members set the priority order each year at a public meetup.

## How recipients are chosen

1. **Eligibility first.** Need-based criteria are published in advance (for example: age, income, medical equipment at home).
2. **Applying is free.** You never have to buy anything, and you don't have to be a customer of any shop.
3. **When there are more eligible applicants than funds**, the order is set by a **public, verifiable random draw** that anyone can re-run and check.

<div class="callout" markdown="1">
**Not a lottery.** Pennsylvania tightly regulates lotteries and games of chance. Because applying is free and requires no purchase, this is designed as a charitable grant program. Counsel will review it before the first grant.
</div>
