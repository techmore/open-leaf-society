### Is this legal?
Yes. Hemp and CBD are legal under federal and Pennsylvania law within THC limits, and those limits tighten on **December 11, 2026**. Open Leaf collects data, writes standards and runs a community fund. We don't sell anything. We follow current law and advocate openly for better law.

### Is this about marijuana?
Mostly not. "Cannabaceae" is the plant family that includes hemp (and hops). We focus on hemp and CBD retailers, growers and processors. Medical cannabis operators are welcome, but the state already oversees them.

### Does the township have to pay for or pass anything?
No. Reports are free. Phase 0 is only conversations.

### Can our township tax hemp sales?
Not directly today. Pennsylvania townships can only levy taxes the state authorizes, and there's no local hemp or cannabis sales tax. What townships can use right now: real local data for planning, voluntary community agreements with member shops, and the 1% Community Dividend, which reaches residents directly.

### Who sees my purchase data?
Nobody outside the shop. Only monthly totals leave the shop, small groups are hidden, and no customer identifiers are ever collected. See [Data]({{ '/data/' | relative_url }}).

### Why ISO 9001?
It's the world's most widely used quality-management standard, and the founders have used it. Our standard borrows its structure (written procedures, records, internal audits, corrective action) so results are **reproducible**: any trained auditor running the same checks gets the same result. The Open Leaf Standard itself is open and free; ISO's own text is not.

### Is the 1% fund a lottery?
No. Applying is free, there's no purchase and no entry fee, and recipients have to meet published need-based criteria. A public random draw only sets the order when there are more eligible applicants than funds. Counsel will review the program before the first grant.

### What's an RFC?
A "Request for Comments": a draft published so anyone can suggest changes before it's final. Our [charter]({{ '/rfc/0001/' | relative_url }}) is RFC-0001. Comment on [GitHub]({{ site.repo }}/issues) or at a meetup.

### Is Open Leaf a nonprofit?
It's forming as one. It isn't incorporated or tax-exempt yet, so contributions aren't tax-deductible yet.

### Does Open Leaf sell products or take a cut of sales?
No. Member dues fund the work. The 1% Community Dividend goes to the community, not to Open Leaf.
