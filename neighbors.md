## What "member shop" means

Member shops agree to all of these, and an independent auditor will check them:

- Every product has a current **lab certificate**, and you can ask to see it.
- **Prices are posted** clearly, with no hidden fees.
- **ID is checked** for age-restricted products, every time.
- Labels say **what's in the package**, including total THC.
- There's a clear way to **raise a complaint**, and complaints are counted in public reports.
- The shop is **independently audited** against an open standard.

## Your privacy

| We publish | We never publish |
|---|---|
| Monthly totals by product category | Your name, phone, email or ID number (we never collect them) |
| Totals by wide area (a township, or the first three digits of a ZIP code), only from customers who opt in | Individual purchases |
| Lab-test and ID-check rates | Anything about a group of 1 to 10 customers |

**About area data:** a member shop may ask for your ZIP code at checkout. Saying no is fine. If you say yes, only the wide area is used for reporting, never your name.

The full rules are on the [data page]({{ '/data/' | relative_url }}).

## Your 1%, without the red tape

Member shops set aside **1% of sales** for the [Community Dividend]({{ '/fund/' | relative_url }}). It goes **directly** to local needs, like battery backup for seniors' homes and support for fire and EMS. It doesn't wait in a township budget line. Neighbors set the priorities once a year at a public meetup, and every dollar is on a public ledger.

## Getting there and home safely

- **Never drive impaired.** We're exploring ride-share partnerships and discounts for members' customers.
- **Shipping and order-ahead pickup** cut trips. Member shops will report how often they're used, so we can see what works.
- **On-site consumption spaces** aren't permitted today. If Pennsylvania law changes, we'll publish a safety standard first.
