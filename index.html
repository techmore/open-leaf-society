Why now

Dec 11, 2026

New federal hemp rules cap total THC at 0.4 mg per package. Local shops are about to change overnight.

No adult-use law

Pennsylvania left cannabis legalization out of the 2026 budget. Townships are left to plan without data.

Demand doesn't vanish

When tested local products disappear, some buyers turn to untested sources with no lab tests and no ID checks.

How it works

  1. Shops opt inMember shops pay dues, agree to the open standard, and put 1% of sales into the Community Dividend.
  2. Records stay at the shopRupert logs sales and batches. Only totals ever leave the store.
  3. Independent auditA separate auditing body will check each shop against the standard.
  4. Public reportsTownships get quarterly reports. Everyone gets the open data.

Meet the plant

Rope that rigged sailing ships. Seeds on grocery shelves. Walls made of hempcrete. An FDA-approved medicine. Tissue cultures that flew to the space station. Pennsylvania even named a township after its hemp fields in 1729. The Cannabaceae family, which includes hops, is one of the most useful groups of plants people have ever grown, and our meetups teach the science behind it, from hops on a trellis to reading a lab report.

The 1% Community Dividend

Member shops put 1% of sales into a community fund. It goes directly to local needs, not through a budget line: battery backup for seniors' homes so heat and medical devices stay on in winter outages, and support for local fire and EMS. Every dollar in and out is posted on a public ledger.
