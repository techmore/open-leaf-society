# Social: Month 1 (Phase 0: Framework & listening)

One post a week. Every post links back to a page on the site. This folder isn't published on the site.

## House rules for every post

- **Educational and civic only.** No prices, products for sale, discounts, "shop now" or store links. Meta, TikTok, X and LinkedIn all restrict cannabis and hemp promotion, and a takedown or account ban costs us the audience.
- **No product close-ups or consumption imagery.** Use text cards, Bucks County scenery, people at meetups (with their permission), charts.
- **Age-gate the accounts** (21+) wherever the platform allows it.
- **Cite facts.** Each factual claim should match something on the site.
- Hashtags (pick 3–4): `#BucksCounty` `#Wrightstown` `#PAhemp` `#OpenData` `#CommunityFirst` `#GroundTruth`

## Week 2: What changes on December 11

**Graphic text:** "Dec 11, 2026: Federal hemp rules change. Here's what that means."

**Caption:**
> On December 11, federal law redefines hemp:
> • THC is measured as *total* THC, including THCA
> • Finished products are capped at 0.4 mg total THC per package
> • Since Nov 12, lab-converted cannabinoids like HHC and THC-O are no longer hemp
>
> For local shops, a large share of shelves changes overnight. For townships, that means the picture from last year no longer applies.
>
> That's why we're building local, open data now, before the change and after it. Full explainer on our Shops page. (Not legal advice; shops should confirm their catalog with counsel.)

**Short:**
> Dec 11: federal hemp rules switch to total THC and a 0.4 mg per-package cap. Here's what changes for Bucks County shops and townships → [link]

**Links to:** /shops/

## Week 3: The 1% idea

**Graphic text:** "1% of every member sale → batteries for seniors' homes, support for fire & EMS."

**Caption:**
> When the power goes out in January, the residents most at risk are older neighbors whose heat, oxygen concentrators and medication fridges depend on electricity.
>
> Open Leaf's proposal: member shops set aside 1% of sales for a Community Dividend. It goes directly to:
> 🔋 Battery backup for seniors' homes
> 🚒 Equipment and training for local fire & EMS
> ⚡ Backup power at shared community sites
>
> Free to apply. Need-based. Every dollar on a public ledger. Help us set the priorities at our first meetup.

**Short:**
> What if 1% of every local hemp sale kept a senior's heat on during an outage? Our Community Dividend proposal, with a public ledger → [link]
