## What "member shop" means

- Every product has a current **lab certificate**, and you can ask to see it.
- **Prices are posted** clearly, with no hidden fees.
- **ID is checked** for age-restricted products, every time.
- Labels say **what's in the package**, including total THC.
- There's a clear way to **raise a complaint**, and complaints are counted in public reports.
- The shop is **independently audited** against an open standard.

## Your privacy

| We publish | We never collect or publish |
|---|---|
| Monthly totals by product category | Your name, phone, email or ID number |
| Totals by area (township or 3-digit ZIP), only if a shop's customers opt in | Individual purchases |
| Lab-testing and ID-check pass rates | Anything about a group smaller than 11 people |

The full rules are on the [data page]({{ '/data/' | relative_url }}).

## Your 1%, without the red tape

Member shops set aside **1% of sales** for the [Community Dividend]({{ '/fund/' | relative_url }}). It goes **directly** to local needs, like battery backup for seniors' homes and support for fire and EMS. It doesn't wait in a township budget line. Neighbors help pick priorities at monthly meetups, and every dollar is on a public ledger.

## Getting there and home safely

- **Never drive impaired.** We're exploring ride-share partnerships and discounts for members' customers.
- **Shipping and order-ahead pickup** cut trips. Member shops will report how often they're used, so we can see what works.
- **On-site consumption spaces** aren't permitted today. If Pennsylvania law changes, we'll publish a safety standard first.
