## A bigger family than you think

The **Cannabaceae** family isn't just cannabis. It also includes:

| Plant | You know it as |
|---|---|
| ***Cannabis sativa*** | Hemp, grown for fiber and seed; and cannabis, grown for medicine |
| ***Humulus lupulus*** | **Hops**, the flower that gives beer its bitterness and aroma |
| ***Celtis occidentalis*** | **Common hackberry**, a tough native shade tree. There may be one on your street. |

## One plant, a thousand jobs

| | |
|---|---|
| 🪢 **Rope & cloth** | Hemp fiber rigged sailing ships and clothed colonists. It's one of the strongest natural fibers. |
| 🌾 **Food** | Hemp seeds are rich in protein and healthy fats. They're sold in grocery stores today. |
| 🧱 **Building** | *Hempcrete*, made from the woody core of the stalk mixed with lime, insulates walls and locks plant carbon into the building. |
| 💊 **Medicine** | The FDA approved a CBD medicine (Epidiolex) for rare forms of childhood epilepsy in 2018. Research on other uses continues. |
| 🔥 **Fuel** | The woody core can be pressed into pellets and is being tested as biomass fuel and animal bedding. |
| 🌱 **Soil cleanup** | Studies, including trials near Chernobyl, suggest hemp roots can pull some heavy metals from contaminated soil. The evidence is promising but still thin. |

## Pennsylvania grew up on hemp

- **1729:** Hempfield Township in Lancaster County was named for the hemp fields there. Lancaster and York counties, along the Susquehanna, were among colonial America's great hemp regions.
- **1800s:** Steamships replaced sails, rope demand fell, and hemp farming moved west.
- **1942:** The U.S. Department of Agriculture released *Hemp for Victory*, urging farmers to grow hemp for the war effort.
- **2019 → today:** Pennsylvania re-opened permitted hemp farming. The state issued 170 growing permits for 2026.

## Hemp in space

In March 2020, hundreds of **hemp tissue cultures** rode a SpaceX cargo flight to the **International Space Station**. It was a private research project by Front Range Biosciences and SpaceCells, run with the University of Colorado's BioServe, to see how microgravity changes the plant's genes and growth. (It wasn't a NASA experiment, but NASA runs the station it flew to.)

## Myth vs. fact

| Heard it? | What's true |
|---|---|
| "Cannabis cleans the air in your house." | **No evidence.** NASA's famous 1989 Clean Air Study tested common houseplants, not cannabis. |
| "Hemp cleans up polluted soil." | **Partly.** Hemp can take up some heavy metals, but results vary, and the plants then have to be handled as contaminated. |
| "Hemp and marijuana are different plants." | **Same species.** The legal difference is how much THC the plant contains. |

## Grow science for homeowners

Good growing is the same science for every plant: soil, light, water, airflow and patience. Our meetups include short, practical workshops on all of it, starting with what you can legally grow today.

| Plant | Can I grow it at home in PA? |
|---|---|
| **Hops** | **Yes.** A climbing vine that loves a sunny trellis or fence. Great for a first grow. |
| **Hackberry** | **Yes.** A native tree that feeds birds and butterflies and handles city conditions. |
| **Hemp** | **Only with a permit** from the PA Department of Agriculture. We found no exception for small home plots. |
| **Cannabis (marijuana)** | **No.** Pennsylvania doesn't allow home growing, including for medical patients. |
